Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of I Love Kanji ("I Love Kanji," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the I Love Kanji mobile application for Android (the "App").

Summary

The App does not require an account and does not show ads. Your learning progress, stories, streaks, settings, purchase receipt, and any photo you choose for the lock screen are stored on your device. The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used, and Google Play Billing for the optional one-time N4–N1 unlock. We do not send the text of your stories or your photos to anyone, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled through the App and through this website. It does not govern the independent privacy practices of Google, device manufacturers, app stores, operating systems, or other third-party services used in connection with the App.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores your learning data locally on your device. This includes your kanji progress, review history, saved stories, streak, preferences, and the purchase receipt used to keep the paid content unlocked.

We do not send the text of your stories to Firebase Analytics or to any server. Analytics only records whether a suggested story was used or a personal story was written, never the story itself. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before the change takes effect.

Lock screen kanji

If you turn on the lock screen kanji feature, the App sets up an Android wallpaper that shows the kanji you are learning. If you choose your own background photo, you select it through Android's built-in photo picker. The App only receives the one photo you select; it does not request access to your photo library or storage. The App copies, rotates, and resizes that photo and saves the copy inside the App's private storage on your device. The photo is never uploaded, shared, or sent to analytics. You can replace it at any time, and it is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

4. Firebase Analytics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to measure how the App is used and to improve its stability, usability, and features. Firebase Analytics processes this information on our behalf.

The App records the following kinds of events: starting or completing a JLPT level, selected N5 learning milestones, completing a story, flashcard, or quiz lesson, whether a suggested or personal story was used (without the story text), reminder screens and the reminder bell, the notification permission result, turning on the lock screen kanji, opening the purchase screen, and purchase attempts, cancellations, and failures. The App does not set your name, email address, or any account identifier as a user ID.

Firebase Analytics also collects certain information automatically, such as first open, sessions, engagement time, app and device information, an app instance ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, how the App was installed (install source), and supported Google Play purchase events.

Advertising ID. The App does not show ads. However, the Firebase Analytics software may access your device's Android Advertising ID. We use this information only for analytics and never for advertising, ad targeting, or ad measurement. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy documentation. See Firebase Privacy and Security, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases and Google Play services

The App offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional learning content through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details, or other payment credentials.

Google Play makes purchase and entitlement information available to the App so that paid content can be unlocked, verified, and restored. The App may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which lets you rate the App without leaving it. That prompt is run by Google; we do not receive your Google account details through it. Google's handling of payment, review, and account information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Notifications and device features

The App asks for Android notification permission only after you choose to turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device, not through an I Love Kanji server. To keep reminders on time, the App reschedules them after a device restart, a time or time zone change, or an App update.

You can turn reminders off in the App or in your device settings. The App also uses vibration for learning feedback, such as answer and completion feedback.

7. Information we do not request

The App does not require an account and does not request access to your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone, camera, precise location, or photo library or storage for its normal use.

8. How information is used

Information described in this Privacy Policy is used to:

  • provide lessons, reviews, streaks, progress tracking, audio, reminders, and the lock screen feature;
  • remember your settings and learning state on your device;
  • process, verify, and restore purchased access through Google Play;
  • measure App usage and improve performance, usability, and features;
  • detect, investigate, and fix technical problems, abuse, or security issues; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable government requests, or to protect legal rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the App.

11. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. The App does not allow Android to back up its data to the cloud, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Data processed by Firebase Analytics and Google Play is retained according to their applicable settings and retention policies. Uninstalling the App resets the Firebase app instance ID, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Because I Love Kanji has no user accounts, there is no account to delete. To remove all data stored by the App, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. For any other privacy request, contact us at the email address below.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the App or this website, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
