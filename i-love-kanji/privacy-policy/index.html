Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the I Love Kanji app for Android (the "app") and this website handle, why, and what you can do about it. "We" and "us" means the developer of I Love Kanji.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Free learners see a few ads from Google AdMob, starting the day after their first finished day. Premium has no ads.
  • Your progress, settings and lock screen photo are saved on your phone.
  • What you draw in the Drawing method is checked on your phone while you write and isn't saved or sent anywhere.
  • If Android backup is on, your phone copies that data into your own Google account backup so you can get it back on a new phone.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to see how the app is used. It never receives your drawings or your photos.
  • Payments for Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your information, and we never see your drawings, photos or answers.

3.What stays on your phone

Everything you do while learning is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We don't run a server that stores your progress, and we can't see it.

In the Drawing method, the strokes you write with your finger are checked on your phone and thrown away when the exercise ends. They're never saved, uploaded or sent to analytics. Build from radicals, Flashcards and Quiz answers work the same way: only whether you got the kanji right goes into your progress. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.

Lock Screen Kanji photo

If you choose your own background photo, you pick it with Android's built-in photo picker. The app only gets the one photo you pick. It doesn't ask for access to your gallery or storage. The app turns the photo upright, makes it smaller, and saves a copy in its own storage. You can change it any time. It's removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app (see section 4 about Android backup).

4.Android backup

The app allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android copies the app's data about once a day to the backup in your own Google account. This includes your progress, settings, lock screen photo and purchase receipts. It's stored as hidden app data, not as files in your Google Drive, and we can't open it.

This lets Android put your progress back if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with the same Google account. Google handles this backup under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off or delete backups in your phone's settings and your Google account settings.

5.Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use and where the app can be better. Google processes this information for us.

The app sends events like these:

  • Learning: starting or finishing a JLPT level, N5 milestones, opening Flashcards, Quiz, Build from radicals or Drawing (and the first time you try each one), finishing a lesson with one of them, and finishing every kanji in a level and moving on to the next one. These events say which method you used, never what you drew or answered.
  • Reminders: your answer to Android's notification permission, the "turn on reminders" screen being shown and your answer, and turning the reminder switch on or off.
  • Purchases: opening the purchase page, starting, cancelling or failing a checkout, a completed purchase or free trial (with the plan), and restored purchases.
  • Ads and streak: a full-screen or rewarded ad being shown, counted or watched to the end, a reward given (+1 freeze or a streak brought back), a Premium learner using a monthly freeze or streak save, tapping a rewarded-ad button, and tapping "No ads".
  • Other: the update pop-up, your answer and whether the update finished downloading and installing, setting Lock Screen Kanji, and resetting your progress.

Firebase also collects some things on its own, such as first open, sessions, time in the app, app updates and removals, device and app information, an app instance ID, rough location from your IP address, how the app was installed, and Google Play purchases. If you're offline, events wait on your phone and are sent later. The app doesn't give Firebase your name, email or any account ID.

Advertising ID. Firebase may read your phone's Android Advertising ID for analytics. Ads use it too (see section 6). You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings.

More from Google: Firebase privacy and security, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

6.Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to free learners, starting the day after their first finished day (the first day is ad-free). Premium (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime) has no ads. There are two kinds:

  • A full-screen ad after each finished study method (up to four a day), before the streak screen, only if it has already loaded. There are no ads in reviews or mistake practice.
  • Optional rewarded ads on the streak card: watch one to get +1 freeze, or to bring a lost streak back with Light it again. The ad is only requested from Google when you tap the button. You only get the reward if Google reports that the ad was watched to the end.

Ads are only requested when your phone is online. To check this, the app contacts Google's connection-check address (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com). That check sends nothing about you except normal connection details such as your IP address. When an ad loads, Google AdMob and the advertising partners Google works with may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address and rough location, phone and app information, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show ads (which may be personalised), measure them and prevent fraud. We never send AdMob your progress, drawings, photos or answers.

Your choice. Where the law asks for consent (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads are personalised. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone).

More from Google: how Google uses information for ads, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

7.Purchases and subscriptions

N5 is free, and the first 10 kanji of N4, N3, N2 and N1 are free to try. The rest of N4 to N1 unlocks with a Monthly or Yearly subscription (Yearly may include a free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase. All payments go through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your card or bank details.

Google Play tells the app which plan you have and gives it a signed receipt, which the app checks on your phone and saves so paid levels work offline. Each time the app opens with an internet connection, it asks Google Play whether your subscription is still active. Prices, sales and free trials shown in the app come from Google Play. Premium also removes ads and gives 3 streak freezes and 1 streak save every month.

8.App updates and ratings

The app uses Google Play to check whether a newer version is available. If you agree in Google Play's update dialog, the update downloads, installs and restarts the app by itself (in the background, or the next time you open the app). It may also show Google Play's rating card after you've learned 15 and 35 kanji. Both are run by Google. We don't get your Google account details from them, and Google doesn't tell us whether you left a rating.

9.Notifications and phone features

  • Notifications. The app only asks for notification permission after you turn reminders on. Reminders (at 12:00 and 19:00 your time) are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server. They use your streak and next kanji, which stay on your phone.
  • Restart and time changes. The app reschedules reminders after your phone restarts, the time or time zone changes, or the app updates.
  • Vibration for answer and lesson feedback.
  • Internet and network state for analytics, ads, Google Play purchases and update checks, and to see whether you're online before an ad is requested.
  • Lock Screen Kanji uses Android's live wallpaper, which you set yourself in Android's wallpaper screen, on the lock screen, the home screen or both. On phones that don't support live wallpapers, the app sets a still picture itself (wallpaper permission) and changes its kanji every 15 minutes from 8:00 to midnight with a quiet, non-waking timer. This happens on your phone; no picture is sent anywhere.

You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

10.What we don't collect

The app doesn't ask for an account, and it doesn't access your contacts, calls, text messages, microphone, camera, precise location, or your photo gallery.

11.How we use information

  • to run lessons in Flashcards, Quiz, Build from radicals and Drawing, reviews, streaks, audio, reminders and Lock Screen Kanji;
  • to remember your settings and progress;
  • to process, check and restore purchases and subscriptions through Google Play;
  • to show ads to free learners and give rewards for rewarded ads;
  • to understand how the app is used and improve it;
  • to find and fix bugs, misuse and security problems;
  • to answer your messages; and
  • to follow the law.

12.Legal bases

Where the law asks for a legal reason to use personal information (for example in the EU and UK), we rely on:

  • contract, to provide the app and anything you buy;
  • legitimate interests, to understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve it, and to show non-personalised ads that keep the free version free, without overriding your rights;
  • consent, for notifications and, where the law requires it, for personalised ads, which you can take back any time; and
  • legal obligations, when the law requires it.

13.Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information for money. For free learners, Google AdMob and its advertising partners receive the ads information in section 6 to show ads. Some US state laws call this "sharing" for targeted advertising; you can opt out with the ad settings on your phone (section 6) or by messaging us. Information is handled by these service providers:

  • Google, for Firebase Analytics, AdMob ads, Google Play purchases, updates and ratings, and Android backup;
  • FormSubmit, which delivers messages you send through the chat on this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety, our rights, or the app.

14.How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Start over: Settings → Reset progress deletes your learned kanji, reviews for every method, mistakes and streak. Your purchase stays.
  • Delete everything on your phone: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Android backup: delete it or turn it off in your phone's backup settings or your Google account.
  • Analytics: event data linked to the app instance ID is kept by Firebase for the period set in our Firebase settings, never longer than 14 months; after that only totals without any ID remain. Uninstalling resets the app instance ID, and you can reset your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Ads: kept by Google AdMob under Google's own retention rules (see Google's links in section 6). Buying Premium stops ads.
  • Support messages: kept as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation, then deleted. Ask us any time to delete them sooner.
  • Subscriptions: cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or in the app under Settings → Manage subscription.

There's no I Love Kanji account, so there's no account to delete. For any other privacy request, send us a message (see section 21).

15.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your information, to limit or object to how it's used, to take back any consent you gave (this doesn't affect what was done before), and to complain to your data protection authority. In Armenia, that is the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Ministry of Justice; in the EU or UK, the authority in your country. In some US states you can also opt out of targeted advertising and ask what we collect.

You don't have to give us any personal information: the app works without an account, a name or an email. We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly important effects. To use a right, message us (section 21). We answer within one month, free of charge.

Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to link analytics data to you. We'll still answer every real privacy request as the law requires.

16.Security

Information sent to Firebase, AdMob and Google Play is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable care to protect information, but no app, network or service can be 100% secure.

17.International transfers

Our service providers, mainly Google, may handle information in other countries than yours, including the United States. Google protects these transfers with safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

18.Children

The app isn't made for children under 13. It doesn't ask for a name, email or account, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you're a parent and think your child sent us personal information, message us and we'll take care of it.

19.Links to other sites

The app and this website link to other websites and services. We're not responsible for what they do with your information.

20.Changes to this policy
