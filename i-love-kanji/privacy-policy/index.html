Privacy Policy

Updated October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the I Love Kanji app for Android (the "app") and this website handle, why, and what you can do about it. "We" and "us" means the developer of I Love Kanji.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up and no ads.
  • Your progress, stories, settings and lock screen photo are saved on your phone.
  • If Android backup is on, your phone copies that data into your own Google account backup so you can get it back on a new phone.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to see how the app is used. It never receives the text of your stories or your photos.
  • Payments for Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your information.

1.What this policy covers

This policy applies to the app and to this website. It doesn't cover how Google, your phone maker, or other companies handle information under their own policies.

3.What stays on your phone

Everything you do while learning is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We don't run a server that stores your progress, and we can't see it.

The text of your stories is never sent anywhere. Analytics only records that you wrote your own story or used the suggested one, never what the story says. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.

Lock Screen Kanji photo

If you choose your own background photo, you pick it with Android's built-in photo picker. The app only gets the one photo you pick. It doesn't ask for access to your gallery or storage. The app turns the photo upright, makes it smaller, and saves a copy in its own storage. You can change it any time. It's removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app (see section 4 about Android backup).

4.Android backup

The app allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android copies the app's data about once a day to the backup in your own Google account. This includes your progress, stories, settings, lock screen photo and purchase receipts. It's stored as hidden app data, not as files in your Google Drive, and we can't open it.

This lets Android put your progress back if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with the same Google account. Google handles this backup under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn backup off or delete backups in your phone's settings and your Google account settings.

5.Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use and where the app can be better. Google processes this information for us.

The app sends events like these:

  • Learning: starting or finishing a JLPT level, N5 milestones, finishing a Story, Flashcard or Quiz lesson, and whether you wrote your own story or used the suggested one (never the story text).
  • Reminders: your answer to Android's notification permission, the "turn on reminders" screen being shown and your answer, and turning the reminder switch on or off.
  • Purchases: opening the purchase page, starting, cancelling or failing a checkout, a completed purchase or free trial (with the plan), and restored purchases.
  • Other: the update pop-up and your answer, setting Lock Screen Kanji, and resetting your progress.

Firebase also collects some things on its own, such as first open, sessions, time in the app, app updates and removals, device and app information, an app instance ID, rough location from your IP address, how the app was installed, and Google Play purchases. If you're offline, events wait on your phone and are sent later. The app doesn't give Firebase your name, email or any account ID.

Advertising ID. The app shows no ads, but Firebase may read your phone's Android Advertising ID. We use it only for analytics, never for ads. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings.

More from Google: Firebase privacy and security, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

6.Purchases and subscriptions

N5 is free. N4 to N1 unlock with a Monthly or Yearly subscription (Yearly may include a free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase. All payments go through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your card or bank details.

Google Play tells the app which plan you have and gives it a signed receipt, which the app checks on your phone and saves so paid levels work offline. Each time the app opens with an internet connection, it asks Google Play whether your subscription is still active. Prices, sales and free trials shown in the app come from Google Play.

7.App updates and ratings

The app uses Google Play to check whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, to download and install it. It may also show Google Play's rating card after you've learned 15 and 35 kanji. Both are run by Google. We don't get your Google account details from them, and Google doesn't tell us whether you left a rating.

8.Notifications and phone features

  • Notifications. The app only asks for notification permission after you turn reminders on. Reminders (at 12:00 and 19:00 your time) are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server. They use your streak and next kanji, which stay on your phone.
  • Restart and time changes. The app reschedules reminders after your phone restarts, the time or time zone changes, or the app updates.
  • Vibration for answer and lesson feedback.
  • Internet for analytics, Google Play purchases and update checks.
  • Lock Screen Kanji uses Android's live wallpaper. You set it yourself in Android's wallpaper screen.

You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

9.What we don't collect

The app doesn't ask for an account, and it doesn't access your contacts, calls, text messages, microphone, camera, precise location, or your photo gallery.

10.How we use information

  • to run lessons, reviews, streaks, audio, reminders and Lock Screen Kanji;
  • to remember your settings and progress;
  • to process, check and restore purchases and subscriptions through Google Play;
  • to understand how the app is used and improve it;
  • to find and fix bugs, misuse and security problems;
  • to answer your messages; and
  • to follow the law.

11.Legal bases

Where the law asks for a legal reason to use personal information (for example in the EU and UK), we rely on:

  • contract, to provide the app and anything you buy;
  • legitimate interests, to understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve it, without overriding your rights;
  • consent, for notifications, which you can take back any time; and
  • legal obligations, when the law requires it.

12.Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. The app has no ad software. Information is handled by these service providers:

  • Google, for Firebase Analytics, Google Play purchases, updates and ratings, and Android backup;
  • FormSubmit, which delivers messages you send through the chat on this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety, our rights, or the app.

13.How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Start over: Settings → Reset progress deletes your learned kanji, stories, reviews and streak. Your purchase stays.
  • Delete everything on your phone: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Android backup: delete it or turn it off in your phone's backup settings or your Google account.
  • Analytics: kept by Firebase according to its retention settings. Uninstalling resets the app instance ID, and you can reset your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Subscriptions: cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or in the app under Settings → Manage subscription.

There's no I Love Kanji account, so there's no account to delete. For any other privacy request, send us a message (see section 20).

14.Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your information, to limit or object to how it's used, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to link analytics data to you. We'll still answer every real privacy request as the law requires.

15.Security

Information sent to Firebase and Google Play is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable care to protect information, but no app, network or service can be 100% secure.

16.International transfers

Our service providers may handle information in other countries than yours, under their own safeguards and the laws that apply to them.

17.Children

The app isn't made for children under 13. It doesn't ask for a name, email or account, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you're a parent and think your child sent us personal information, message us and we'll take care of it.

18.Links to other sites

The app and this website link to other websites and services. We're not responsible for what they do with your information.

19.Changes to this policy

When the app or the law changes, we'll update this policy and change the "Last updated" date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app or on the Google Play page.
