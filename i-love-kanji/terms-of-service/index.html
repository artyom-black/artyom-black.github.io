6.Subscriptions and free trials

  • Auto-renewal. Monthly and Yearly renew automatically at the end of each period, and Google Play charges you, until you cancel.

  • Offline. The app checks your subscription with Google Play when it's online. Without internet, paid levels stay open for up to 7 days after the last successful check.
  • Price changes. If a subscription price changes, Google Play will tell you beforehand as its rules require.

7.Lifetime purchase

Lifetime is a one-time payment that unlocks N4–N1 for as long as we offer and support the app, on any phone signed in to the same Google account. It isn't a subscription. If you have a subscription and buy Lifetime, remember to cancel the subscription in Google Play so it doesn't renew.
